Most business dashboards fail for one simple reason: they report past symptoms instead of isolating root causes and driving future decisions. During my Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Internship at Datheon, I set out to bridge the gap between diagnostic data engineering, predictive machine learning, and CFO-level decision modeling. I engineered the RISK Decision Intelligence Platform — an autonomous, evidence-driven analytics engine solving 4 critical commercial challenges across Retail, Subscriber Retention, Financial Modeling, and Power BI. Here is what happened when we applied rigorous data intelligence to real business data: 🔍 1. Retail Sales: Unmasking the "Discount Trap" • The Symptom: A seemingly healthy $2.30M in top-line retail sales. • The Root Cause: Products sold with discounts exceeding 20% caused an aggregate -$135,376.06 loss with a disastrous -37.32% margin. • The Action: Pinpointed that the "Tables" subcategory alone leaked -$17,725.48 across 319 transactions. Implemented hard discount ceiling rules and warranty bundles to protect margin integrity. 🏛️ 2. Power BI: Star Schema & Zero-Dollar Variance • Built a high-performance relational dimensional model: FactSales (9,994 records) connected to DimProduct (1,862 SKUs), DimCustomer (793 customers), DimGeography (631 postal codes), and a continuous 1,464-day DimDate calendar table. • Developed a complete production DAX Time Intelligence suite (Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, YoY Growth, Sales YTD). • The Rigor: Reconciled every single DAX measure against Python down to the exact penny ($0.00 variance). 📉 3. Customer Retention: Isolating the "Toxic Triad" • The Discovery: Churn is heavily tenure-dependent — new subscribers suffer an alarming 47.44% churn rate in months 0–12, dropping to just 6.61% after year 5. • The Toxic Triad: Subscribers on Month-to-Month contracts + Fiber Optic service + Electronic Check payments experience a staggering 60.37% churn rate. • The Strategy: Trained and calibrated predictive classifiers (0.8418 ROC-AUC) to trigger automated 90-day onboarding concierges and a $10 autopay switch credit, cutting churn hazard by over 50%. 💼 4. Financial Modeling: The What-If Sensitivity Simulator • The Problem: The Enterprise segment generated $19.61M in gross sales but operated at a net loss of -$614,531.20. • The Solution: Built an interactive multi-variate scenario engine. A targeted 10% discount adjustment restores +$920,524.82 directly to EBITDA, while Channel Partners delivered a premium 73.13% profit margin. ⚙️ How It Was Built: The 4-Stage RISK Framework • R — Reconnaissance: Strict schema auditing, boundary checks, and zero-data-loss ingestion. • I — Intelligence: Diagnostic slicing, cohort hazard modeling, and root cause isolation. • S — Solution: Star-schema relational engineering, calibrated machine learning, and sensitivity simulators. • K — KPI Verification: 21 automated pytest test suites and executive decision ledgers. 📊 Tech Stack: Python | Pandas | Scikit-Learn | Power BI | DAX | Pytest | Jupyter Lab | Git Check out the full repository, code, star schema, and live interactive portal: 👉 GitHub: https://github.com/vijaymahes9080/Datheon_Data_Analytics_-_Power_BI_Internship #DataAnalytics #PowerBI #BusinessIntelligence #DataScience #MachineLearning #Python #DAX #FinancialModeling #CustomerRetention #AnalyticsEngineering #PortfolioProject #Datheon
